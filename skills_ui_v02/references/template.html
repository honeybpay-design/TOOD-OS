One loop, from first message to shipped fix

Loopwright follows a request through four moves. Scroll through them, or pick the one your team struggles with.

Capture

Every source lands in one inbox

Connect the widget and a forwarding address in about ten minutes. Loopwright merges duplicates and attaches each message to the account that sent it.

  • Eleven native sources, plus a webhook for the rest
  • An 18 KB widget that loads after your app does
  • Imports the last twelve months of tickets
Understand

Themes rank themselves

Every message is tagged against your taxonomy and scored for sentiment. Themes rank by volume and by the revenue asking, not by who spoke last in the meeting.

  • Your own taxonomy, not a generic one
  • Week-over-week change on every theme
Prioritize

Decide with the accounts in view

Link themes to roadmap items. Each card shows how many customers are waiting and what they are worth, synced both ways with your issue tracker.

  • Two-way status sync with your issue tracker
  • ARR and renewal dates on every card
Follow up

Tell everyone when it ships

When the linked issue closes, Loopwright drafts a personal note to every customer who asked. You review it once, and it goes out from your own domain.

  • Merge fields from the original request
  • Replies thread back into the inbox

        Customer story
        
How Fernhold cut weekly triage from nine hours to fifty minutes

        
Fernhold builds project planning software for construction firms. Its six-person product team had plenty of feedback and no way to trace it back to customers.

        
      

      

        

          
Company
Fernhold

          
Industry
Construction software

          
Team
6 in product, 14 in support

          
Plan
Growth, since March 2026

          
Sources
Help desk, widget, email, call notes

          
Region
EU data residency

        

        

          
Feedback everywhere, evidence nowhere

          
Before Loopwright, requests reached Fernhold through four help desk queues, a shared inbox and the sales team's call notes. Amara Okafor's team spent most of every Monday copying them into a spreadsheet, and by Thursday it was already out of date.

          
“We knew customers wanted better exports,” Amara says. “We did not know it was 38 accounts worth more than half a million dollars a year. That number changed the conversation with engineering overnight.”

          
Two afternoons of setup

          
Fernhold connected its help desk and the in-app widget first, imported twelve months of tickets, and wrote a taxonomy of 42 themes together with the support leads. In the first month AI tagging handled 91 percent of new items, and the rest went to a fifteen-minute daily triage.

          
          

            
Every roadmap item now shows the accounts asking for it. Prioritization went from an hour of opinions to ten minutes of evidence.

            
Amara OkaforHead of Product, Fernhold

          

          
A roadmap customers can see themselves in

          
Each roadmap card now carries the accounts behind it. When the CSV export fix shipped in September, a loop digest went to all 38 customers the same morning, and seven of them replied to say thanks or ask for the next thing.

Legal and security

      
Plain-language summaries of how Loopwright handles your data. Last updated September 12, 2026.

      

        

          PrivacyTermsSecuritySubprocessors
        

        

          

            
Privacy

            
You own the feedback you bring into Loopwright. We process it only to run the product for your workspace, and we never sell it or use it to train models shared with other customers.

            
What we collect

            
  • Account details for the people on your team: name, work email and role.
  • Feedback content and metadata from the sources you connect.
  • Product usage events, used to improve Loopwright and kept for 13 months.

            
Your choices

            
Admins can export or delete a workspace at any time. Deleted data leaves our primary systems within 30 days and our backups within 90.

          

          

            
Terms

            
Plans renew at the end of each billing period until cancelled. You can downgrade or cancel from workspace settings, and you keep access until the period ends.

            
  • Fair use: automated sources count toward your monthly feedback items.
  • Uptime: Enterprise agreements include a 99.9 percent monthly uptime commitment.
  • Support: Growth and Enterprise plans receive a first reply within four business hours.

          

          

            
Security

            
Loopwright is audited against SOC 2 Type II every year. Workspaces are hosted in the US, or in the EU for Growth and Enterprise plans, and data does not move between regions.

            
  • AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.2 or newer in transit.
  • SSO with SAML 2.0, SCIM provisioning and enforced session timeouts.
  • Role-based permissions and an audit log of every export and permission change.
  • Annual third-party penetration tests, with summaries shared under NDA.
