Riverside Council: Digital Services Tender

Instructions to Tenderers

Tenders must be submitted no later than 12:00 on 14 November 2026 through the council portal. Late tenders will be rejected.

Tenderers must attach a copy of their current Cyber Essentials certificate. Tenderers must also provide a copy of their information security policy.

Tenderers must confirm that they are not subject to any exclusion grounds. This declaration is mandatory.

Question 1: Mobilisation (20%)

Describe your approach to mobilisation, including how you will transfer services from the incumbent supplier. Your response must not exceed 750 words. This question carries a weighting of 20%.

Question 2: Information Security (15%)

Explain how you protect council data and demonstrate your relevant certifications. Responses should not exceed 500 words. This question carries a weighting of 15%.

Pass/fail: Tenderers who do not hold Cyber Essentials will fail this stage and will be rejected.
