    Questions

    Asked plainly. Answered plainly.

    Is it allowed to use AI to write a public-sector bid?

    Yes. Cabinet Office guidance states that suppliers' use of AI is not prohibited, but authorities may ask you to disclose it and to confirm the output was checked for accuracy. See PPN 017.

    Where is my data processed?

    The product is designed to run in the UK (AWS London), with AI inference routed only to providers and regions that meet our residency rules. Your documents are never used to train models. The public demo on this page processes files in memory and does not store them.

    Which AI models do you use?

    Only models that have passed our admission tests. We are provider-neutral; the admitted list and model cards will be published in our trust centre.

    What happens to text I paste from elsewhere?

    It is labelled “pasted, origin unknown” and counted separately in the AI use record. We do not guess where it came from.

    What do founding members commit to?

    Using tood.os on at least one real bid, and a short feedback call each month. In return you keep founding pricing for 12 months and shape the roadmap.
