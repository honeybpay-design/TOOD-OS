## Compliance positioning (hero + navbar badge)
- **Primary claim:** "AI Top-tier Agent — Built for UK Public Sector Procurement"
- **Compliance badges:** Procurement Act 2023 · PPN 01/17 · PPN 02/24
- **PPN 01/17** — Social Value in procurement (must demonstrate social value in bids)
- **PPN 02/24** — Artificial Intelligence in procurement (AI use must be transparent, auditable, human-approved)
- **Procurement Act 2023** — replaced PCR 2015, new rules for public contracts from Feb 2025
- These are not marketing claims — they describe what TOOD is built to comply with
- Use in: hero tagline, compliance section, pricing (Enterprise tier especially)
